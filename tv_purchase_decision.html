During Installation (Day 1)

  • Power-On Test: Keep TV powered for 15 minutes to warm up panel before testing.

On-Site Panel Testing (Before Signing)

  • Test 1 - Dead Pixels: Display solid Red, Green, Blue, White, Black slides. Scan within 2 feet for tiny black or colored spots.
  • Test 2 - Panel Uniformity: Display 50% grey slide. Check for cloudy patches or dark bands (Dirty Screen Effect).
  • Test 3 - Local Dimming: Play white square moving on black background in dark room. Verify no severe blooming halos.
  • Test 4 - Physical Integrity: Check bezel alignment, HDMI port rigidity, no hairline cracks.
  • Test 5 - Chassis Resonance: Play bass-heavy audio at 60% volume. Verify no internal buzzing or rattling.
⚠️ If Any Test Fails: Immediately request DOA (Dead on Arrival) log from technician on their digital service portal. Do NOT accept promises of "future software patches" for physical panel defects. Once you sign completion report, your leverage shifts to warranty dispute.
